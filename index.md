# The Nurse Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 17, 2026
**Effective date:** May 17, 2026

ASKIR Games ("we," "our," or "us") operates the The Nurse mobile application (the "Game"). This Game was developed using the Unreal Engine 5.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information.

### 1. Information Collection and Use
We do not collect, store, use, or share any personal data or personal information from users of our Game. The Game does not request any sensitive permissions (such as access to your camera, contacts, microphone, or precise location).

### 2. Third-Party Sharing and Disclosure
We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise transfer your personal information or data to any third parties. Since we do not collect any user data, it is impossible for us to share or disclose it to any external companies, advertisers, or organizations.

### 3. Third-Party Services
The Game integrates with **Google Play Games Services** to provide features such as achievements, leaderboards, and cloud saves. 

While we do not collect or share your data, Google Play Games Services may collect and process information in accordance with Google's own Privacy Policy. This data may include your Google Play Games player ID, game progress, and system performance logs.

You can read more about how Google handles your data here:
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### 4. Unreal Engine 5
The Game is built using Unreal Engine 5. The engine itself does not collect any personally identifiable information from you through our Game.

### 5. Children's Privacy
Our Game does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
